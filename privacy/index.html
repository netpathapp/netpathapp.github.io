Privacy Policy

Your money stays on your phone.

Last updated 6 October 2026

The short version
  • No account, no login, no cloud sync. Every account, balance and transaction you enter is stored only in NetPath's local storage on your device.
  • We never see it, back it up, or have a way to retrieve it — if you delete the app, it's gone, unless you exported a backup yourself.
  • Looking up a live crypto or stock price sends only a ticker symbol to Binance or Yahoo Finance — never your holdings, your name, or anything that identifies you.
  • The only thing that ever leaves your device tied to you is an email address, and only if you choose to type one into the optional "Stay in touch" field in Settings.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, nothing sold to anyone.

1. The data that never leaves your device

NetPath has no backend server for your financial data and no user accounts. Everything you enter — accounts, balances, transactions, notes, tax settings, your preferences — is written to your device's local storage and read back only by the app, only on that device. There is no sync, no cloud backup, and no mechanism in the app for that data to reach us or anyone else.

If you use the Export data option in Settings, NetPath builds a JSON file on your device and hands it to your phone's own share sheet — where it goes next (AirDrop, email, a cloud drive) is entirely your choice, made outside the app.

The monthly report works the same way: NetPath builds the PDF on your device from your local data and hands it to your phone's share sheet. It is only ever saved or sent where you choose to put it.

If you turn on reminders, NetPath asks your phone's operating system to show a notification on a schedule (for example on the 1st of each month). The schedule lives on your device and is run by the system — no server is involved, and no notification token is created or sent anywhere.

If you enable Face ID lock, authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system. NetPath asks "was this person verified?" and gets a yes or no — it never receives your fingerprint, face data, or anything biometric.

2. Live price lookups

If you turn on Live prices in Settings, NetPath periodically asks two public, third-party price services for current values. Each request sends only what's needed to look up a price — never your quantities, your account names, or any way to tie the request back to you.

ServiceUsed forWhat's sent
Binance public API Crypto prices (BTC, ETH, SOL and others) A trading pair symbol (e.g. "BTCEUR") and nothing else
Yahoo Finance Stock, ETF and commodity prices, and ticker search A ticker symbol, or your search text when you look one up, and nothing else

Neither service receives your name, device ID, holdings, or any other identifying information from NetPath.

3. The one optional exception: email updates

Settings includes an optional, clearly separate "Stay in touch" field. If — and only if — you type in an email address and tap Subscribe, that address is sent to our database (hosted on Supabase) and used solely to occasionally email you about new features or updates. It is stored in a table completely separate from app functionality; nothing about your accounts, balances, or usage is attached to it, because nothing about those is ever sent off your device in the first place.

You can ask us to delete this email at any time — see the contact box at the bottom of this page.

4. What we don't do

  • No bank or brokerage account connections, and no third-party financial data aggregators (e.g. Plaid). You type in your own numbers.
  • No analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or behavioral trackers of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no ad identifiers collected.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of any data with data brokers or advertisers.
  • No account system — there's nothing to log into, so there's no password or profile tied to you.

5. Data retention and deletion

On your device  Your financial data lives for as long as the app is installed, or until you use Delete all data in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes it entirely. We have no copy and no way to recover it for you.

Newsletter email  Kept until you ask us to remove it, or until you unsubscribe via a link in any email we send.

6. Your rights

Because your financial data never leaves your device, you already hold it — there's nothing for us to export, correct, or hand over on request, since we never had a copy. For the one thing we do hold (an optional newsletter email), you can ask us at any time to tell you what we have, correct it, or delete it, under GDPR or any equivalent local law that applies to you.

7. Children's privacy

NetPath isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

8. This website

The NetPath website is a static page. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and it serves its own fonts and images instead of loading them from third parties. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs; we don't receive or use that data.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters — a new third-party service, a new type of data collected — we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Since the app has no way to contact you directly, we'd encourage checking back here occasionally if that matters to you.
